Day 1

Buena Park → Oakhurst

Yosemite setup day

A simple first leg that positions everyone close to Yosemite’s south entrance.

Head toward Yosemite country

Keep this day easy: drive, dinner, settle in.

Hampton Inn Oakhurst-Yosemite

Predictable, close to the park, and perfect for an early start.

What to know

  • Pack park bags separately.
  • Early bedtime matters.

Day 2

Yosemite → Tehachapi

Cane-friendly Yosemite highlights, then a flow-through drive toward the next destination.

Tunnel View

Iconic view with very little walking.

Bridalveil Fall

Safer waterfall option. Go only as far as feels good.

Base Camp Eatery

Easy seating and vegetarian-friendly options.

Hampton Inn Tehachapi

Quiet, practical overnight.

What to know

  • This is not a full Yosemite day.
  • The win is seeing Yosemite without exhausting everyone.

Day 3

Tehachapi → Las Vegas

Transit day. Vegas is a pass-through stop, not the destination.

Hampton Inn & Suites Las Vegas Red Rock

Off-strip and quieter.

Drive the Strip

Show the kids the lights from the car. No parking, no walking.

What to know

  • Keep Vegas controlled and simple.
  • Eat near the hotel.

Day 4

Las Vegas → Page via Zion

Drive through Zion west-to-east using the America the Beautiful Lifetime Senior Pass.

Zion-Mount Carmel Highway

Scenic drive-through, tunnel, canyon views, and optional pull-offs.

Hampton Inn & Suites Page - Lake Powell

Sets up Condor Day.

What to know

  • Skip main canyon shuttles.
  • Let Zion be a drive-through treasure.

Day 5 · Condor Day

Page → Navajo Bridge → Page

The emotional highlight: California condors at Navajo Bridge.

Navajo Bridge

Flat pedestrian bridge, cane-friendly, with a real chance to see condors. Bring binoculars.

Lunch near Marble Canyon Lodge

Simple, nearby, low-stress.

Page again

Rest, pool, laundry, and a calm evening.

What to know

  • Do not rush this stop.
  • Morning is best.

Day 6

Page → Albuquerque

Pueblos day, centered around Acoma Pueblo / Sky City Cultural Center.

Acoma Pueblo / Sky City Cultural Center

The cultural center is enough. Guided tour only if walking and energy allow.

Hampton Inn Albuquerque area

Easy overnight before turning north.

What to know

  • Respect photography rules.
  • This is a living community.

Day 7

Albuquerque → Trinidad

Bonus Colorado day with a changing landscape.

Optional Capulin Volcano

Drive to the top if timing works.

Hampton Inn Trinidad

Small-town Colorado stop.

What to know

  • Elevation increases.
  • Expect cooler air.

Day 8 · Cowboy Day

Trinidad → Dodge City

Maverick’s cowboy day. Save the outfit for Boot Hill.

Boot Hill Museum

Old West backdrop and a perfect Maverick memory.

Hampton Inn & Suites Dodge City

Easy overnight after Cowboy Day.

Maverick note

  • Pack the outfit where it is easy to grab.
  • Let him lead for a few minutes.

Day 9

Dodge City → Little Rock

Longer travel day

This is the long push day that turns the route south and east toward Perry.

Through Wichita, then south/east

Wichita is only the turn point, not an overnight stop.

Diner or simple road lunch

Use easy vegetarian-friendly options: omelet, grilled cheese, salad, pancakes, or sides.

Hampton Inn Little Rock area

End the long day with a predictable hotel.

What to know

  • This is the day to start early.
  • Plan two short breaks.
  • Keep dinner very simple.

Day 10

Little Rock → Nashville

A more manageable drive day after the long push.

Arkansas to Tennessee

Let this be steady and uncomplicated.

Hampton Inn Nashville area

Choose easy access over downtown chaos.

What to know

  • No need to sightsee Nashville.
  • This is a recovery-and-positioning day.

South toward Georgia

Final stretch home.

Georgia to North Carolina

Goldsboro

Home. The story closes here.
